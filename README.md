# Single-Photon-Scanning-Imaging-for-Space-Situational-Awareness
This project will develop a proof of concept single photon scanning imaging system for Space Situational Awareness (SSA), addressing the limitations of conventional camera sensors that do not provide time-resolved, photon level sensitivity in extremely low light regimes. The system concept replaces a focal plane array with a fibre coupled single photon detector, where the fibre tip is scanned across the telescope focal plane using a precision XY motion stage driven by piezoelectric actuators; sequential photon counts are then processed through a reconstruction algorithm to form a two dimensional image of the sky. The primary aims are to design and model the mechanical scanning system, acquire  photon limited image acquisition, and develop a reconstruction algorithm capable of recovering spatial information from sparse, noisy data. Intended outcomes include a complete 3D CAD design of the scanning head, a validated simulation of the scanning and detection process, a working image reconstruction code, and a system level assessment of performance for detecting faint satellites and debris. The project will provide hands-on experience in mechanical design, opto-mechanical integration, image processing, and programming in environments such as MATLAB or Python, making it particularly suitable for students interested in optical engineering, photonics, space technologies, and instrumentation. Applicants with some familiarity in programming and basic optics or mechanics may apply. The work is primarily design and construction with 3D printing and laboratory validation.  The project  will contribute to the development of next-generation, high-sensitivity sensing approaches for SSA.
